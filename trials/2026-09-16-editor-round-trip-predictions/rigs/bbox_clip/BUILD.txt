bbox_clip -- boundingbox and clipping attachments, the clipping end slot and convex flag, both editor colours

rig spec name: r6bbox

Built green on 2026-09-16 against the rigc worktree at d8485ea. Two commands,
in this order, both run from the rigc checkout root:

  # 1. the plates. Checkerboards with PLACEHOLDER burned in, drawn by
  #    tools/plate.ts. Nothing here is art and no claim about appearance can
  #    come from any of it.
  RIGC_ROOT=<rigc checkout> bun rigs/bbox_clip/r6_make_plates.ts

  # 2. the build. rigc emits only after green, so a written skeleton.json IS
  #    the gate having passed.
  bun cli.ts build \
    --rig    rigs/bbox_clip/rig.json \
    --motion rigs/bbox_clip/motion.json \
    --out    out/bbox_clip/build \
    --copy-images

run-all.sh runs exactly these two, then tools/editor_roundtrip.ts, then read.ts.

Why --copy-images: the round-trip harness refuses a build whose atlas names its pages
by path, because it copies the atlas beside the export at another depth and
every page name would resolve to nothing. --copy-images puts the pages beside the skeleton under plain names.
