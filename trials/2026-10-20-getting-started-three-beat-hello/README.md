# 2026-10-20 getting-started-three-beat-hello: a hello on the Getting Started rig with no new art

## Question

Can a three-beat "hello" (a "hm?" look, a "heh" eye-smile with a shoulder
bob, a wave) plus a weight shift be keyed on the rig that the
[Getting Started guide](https://github.com/rig-c/getting-started) builds
from its step-2 painting, with no new art, so that `rig-parts build` passes
and `rigc render --seam` / `--pass-through` stay inside the bars below on
every frame?

## Verdict

**PARTIAL** — Every one of the three beats can be keyed with no new art, in
the form the art allows. The "hm?" is a head roll of up to 6° with a lean.
The "heh" is a narrowed-eye smile (`eyes` scaley 0.70, mouth widened) with two
4 px shoulder lifts. The wave is elbow-down: the forearm swings anywhere from
−160° to +60° at the elbow, and the upper arm stays within 4° of rest. The full
4 s `hello` builds green through the producer (gate green, no folds,
`check: PASS`). All 14 seam pairs that can be read stay within gap ≤ 25 /
ghost ≤ 50, and the skirt passes through a thigh on 0 frames. The 15th pair,
`shoes | shin_l`, is a SKIP because the two drawings share no band. What does
not pass is a real wave or a real weight shift. Moving the upper arm more
than 4° opens the shoulder: 50 px at −6°, and 1070–3772 px at the 25–100 %
swings of the guide's own wave. The weight shift passes only at 6 px (0.5 %
of the figure), below the 1–2 % asked for, and the foot cannot lift.

## Rejected alternatives

- **Handing `rig-pose retarget`'s output to `motion.animations_from` as
  written.** Refused at assemble: four `CONFIG_KEY_KNOWN` failures
  (`archetype`, `cut`, `easings`, `groups` are not fields there) and one
  `CONFIG_ANIMATION_NAME_FREE` (`animations.idle` is the idle the config
  writes). The result was `refused: 5 problem(s)`. Keeping only
  `{"spec", "animations": {"wave": …}}` would pass, but rig-pose carries
  body-18 and no face, so it cannot write three of the beats.
- **The overlay path: extend retarget's file by hand and compile it with
  `rigc build`.** Two tracks were appended (`head` rotate 0 → 6 → 6 → 0,
  `eye_r` translate to [1.5, 0]). `rigc build --rig out/rig/rig.json
  --motion …` exited 0 with `52 assertions: 19 measured (19 passed, 0 failed),
  19 skipped, 14 not in profile "spine"`. It is rejected for the guide because
  it skips `rig-parts check`, so nothing reads it for folds or seams.
- **An overhead wave on this art.** Every swing of the guide's own wave from
  25 % to 100 % opens the shoulder by 1070–3772 px, against a gap bar of 25
  (table above). Stretching the region is not a fix. The armhole it uncovers
  was never painted.
- **A head turn toward the viewer (yaw).** The guide's `propose --keypoints`
  drafts no `turn` block, so this rig has no turn control. On this rig "hm?"
  is head roll. `head_up` was considered and rejected: it is a slotless bone
  that draws nothing, so keying it moves nothing.
- **Moving the eyes to the viewer with a key.** The iris has no bone of its
  own. It rides `eye_*` with the eyewhite and lash, so an `eye_*` translate
  moves the whole eye. The painted irises already face the viewer.
- **An eye-smile by attachment swap.** There is no second lash or eyewhite
  image to swap to. The narrowing (`eyes` scaley 0.70) squashes lash, eyewhite
  and iris together about the eyewhite's centre. It reads as narrowed eyes,
  not as a lower lid rising.
- **A larger weight shift, and a skirt that follows it.** Every amplitude
  from 6 to 37 px fails at the left thigh's hem, with the skirt links
  following or not (table above). The safe amplitude is 6 px with the links
  following.
- **The weight shift as the opener.** It was placed last, as the settle,
  because in beats 1–2 the eye is on the face. This placement was judged, not
  measured. At 6 px the shift can be dropped with nothing lost.

## Limits

- **One painting, one rig, one set of pins.** The figures belong to the
  guide's painting as rigged by parts 1.42.0 with the guide's
  `propose --keypoints`. Another painting, or a rig proposed with
  `--full`/`--head`, has different joints.
- **Six moves need more than keys on this rig.** The record lists them as
  needing a painted part. Two of them need configuration rather than new art,
  and that is marked on each:
  1. **A true eye-smile** (∩ lids, the lower lid rising): a closed-curve lash
     and lid image per eye, carried as an alternate attachment. *New art.*
  2. **The eyes moving to the viewer inside the eye:** the iris on its own bone
     (config `bones` and `regions`) and the eyewhite as a clip. *No new art
     by the record's own account; not measured.* Without a clip, an iris shift
     spills over the eyewhite's edge.
  3. **A raised-arm wave** (upper arm out more than 4°): a raised-arm painting
     set, with the puff sleeve and armhole painted at that angle and switched
     by an attachment key (`assemble.alternates`). *New art.*
  4. **A head turn toward the viewer (yaw):** `config.turn`, drafted by
     `propose --full`/`--head` and rated to ±15° on one face plate. *No new
     art within ±15°.* Beyond that it needs a second face plate, which is new
     art.
  5. **A visible weight shift (1–2 %) and a lifted free foot:** thigh art
     that continues under the skirt (or the skirt on physics), and the shoes
     split per foot onto the shin bones. *New art for the thigh.* The guide's
     README, as read on 2026-10-23, now cuts the shoes into `foot_r` and
     `foot_l` through `propose` with a corrected keypoint, so the shoe half
     no longer needs art on later parts versions. This trial did not measure
     that.
  6. **Visible brow motion:** the brows sit under the fringe (`hair_front`
     draws last), so a brow key barely shows. It needs a fringe cut or brows
     drawn over the fringe. *New art.*
- **Side finding: `retarget` refuses the guide's un-cleaned pose.** On the
  run date, the guide's step 9 ran `rig-pose retarget` on `wave.pose.json`
  with no `clean` step. rig-pose 0.36.0 refuses that and exits 1, verbatim:

  ```
  FAIL  POSE_NOT_CLEANED: wave.pose.json has no "cleaned" record; retarget reads a sequence rig-pose clean wrote (spikes, occlusion, smoothing; there is no flag to skip it). Run `rig-pose clean --pose wave.pose.json --out wave.pose.clean.json` (add --loop for one cycle of a gait) and retarget --pose wave.pose.clean.json
  ```
