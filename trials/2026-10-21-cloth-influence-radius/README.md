# 2026-10-21 cloth-influence-radius: which influence radius keeps cloth from folding

## Question

Is there a value of `meshes.<part>.influence.radius`, drafted by `rig-parts
propose --cloth` as *farthest + margin*, that keeps every covered cloth and
hair part at 0 folded frames under wind and a hip sway, with `rig-parts check`
green, on the public `wind` figure and on the generated cloth fixture? The
answer is "partly". The question comes from rig-c/parts#513.

The same day, a follow-up run on the same two subjects measured whether
smoothing the fork shares, capped at 8 influences or at 6, changes wind's
`bottomwear` fold frames under the 60° sway. This is rig-c/parts#615, and it
is reported in its own part of the Measurement section.

## Measurement

### Thresholds, stated in the brief before the sweep ran

- **Bar 1 (folds)**: 0 folded frames on every covered part under the wind
  stimulus and the 8° and 30° sways.
- **Bar 2 (check)**: `rig-parts check` exits 0 on both subjects, under the wind
  schedule.
- **Bar 3**: under the 60° sway, rig-c/parts#378's bar IF04 asks that the
  influence mesh fold no more than the part's own lattice.

A folded frame is a frame in which `rigc render --folds` reports at least one
folded triangle on the part's slot, at 30 fps with physics on. `check` reads
its own idle, not the sways.

The farthest distance is the tree's own figure: the fixture draft's
`influence-radius` LINT finding (`influenceFarthest`), and the same LINT line
in wind's `propose` output. The radius is swept in three forms:
*farthest + m px* (m = 1–128), *farthest × (1 + s)* (s = 0.05–3), and
*farthest + n × cell* (n = 0.5–11). That is 23 values plus the lattice, on 6
parts, for 144 rows in `results/sweep.csv`. The full sweep table is
`results/sweep.md`, and the raw per-variant results, including each mesh's
influences and reach, are in `results/*-results.json`.

The bar for this follow-up was ≤ the lattice's 5. With cap 8, wind's
`bottomwear` goes from 41 to 3 when smoothing alone is applied, and to 0 when
`max` is raised to 8 and smoothed. Capped at 6 it reads 23, above the bar.

The second run shows the first run's reading was wrong. "Smoothing closes
41 → 3" credits the smoothing with what raising `max` to 8 does on its own
(3, unsmoothed). Smoothing takes it from 3 to 0. On the fixture skirt with
`hold_tip` on, the second run reads 20 before and 0 after, and 0 with `max`
8 unsmoothed (`smoothing/pr-branch-sweep.log`).

## Verdict

**PARTIAL**. With the radius at +2 or +3 cells beyond the farthest vertex,
every covered part on both subjects reads 0 folded frames under wind, 8° and
30°, with `check` green. Two things fail. From +4 cells, `CHECK_TIP_OVER_ROOT`
fails on wind/bottomwear (ratio 1.471 against ≥ 1.4725). At the 60° sway no
swept radius reads 0 on every part, and wind/bottomwear folds on 32–88 frames
against 5 for its lattice. At that sway, smoothing capped at 8 reaches 0 only
once `max` is raised to 8 (3 frames unsmoothed), and capped at 6 it reads 23.

## Rejected alternatives

- **Absolute px or share of the distance as the drafting rule.** Each passes
  at exactly one swept value (+64 px; share 1), with failing values on both
  sides, so neither has measured slack. The px rule's needed margin also
  spread 12–64 px across parts in the first reading (below).
- **A first reading of the same question on 2026-10-19** (`rig-c/parts` 1.41.0,
  rigc 2.59.0, 8 parts, 45 variants, wind + 8° + 30° only, `check` not run).
  It found +2 to +6 cells all at 0 folds and a fold returning at 8–11 cells.
  It led to the +3-cell draft (#518 → #522). It was superseded rather than
  extended, because it measured neither the check nor the 60° sway, and those
  are the two halves that fail here. Its eight sheets are not committed: they

## Limits

- **Two subjects and six parts.** The window's lower edge (+2 cells) rests on
  one part, wind/hair_front. The ceiling (+4 cells) rests on one part and one
  check, wind/bottomwear's `CHECK_TIP_OVER_ROOT`.
- **The sweep is discrete.** The true edges lie between swept values: the
  floor between +1 and +2 cells, and the ceiling between +3 and +4.
- **Only `max: 6, across: true, shape: "smooth"`** at each part's lattice cell
  was swept. The window may move with any of those.
- **The wind stimulus folded nothing** on any variant of either subject, so
  only the hip sways discriminate.
- **`check` reads the idle under the wind schedule, not the sways.** Bar 2
  says nothing about the swayed poses.
- **Nothing was judged by eye.** Folds are counted by `rigc render --folds`,
  and no render was looked at.
- **Recording gaps.** The command lines of the wind `assemble` and `propose`
  steps, the Bun version and the #615 harness scripts were not recorded or
  kept. The `rig-parts` version 1.52.0 is the run's own label, and only the
  commit was checked.
- **The follow-up's two runs used different trees** (`5db9fba` plus a patch;
  1.54.0 against the #622 branch). The figures shared by both runs agree
  (lattice 5, draft 41, cap 6 = 23).
