directory is dated by its start.

## Question

Can `rig-parts` (`@rig-c/parts`) rig the Getting Started character so that a short beat
brings its feet to a parallel "11" stance and back — with every leg seam inside the bars
below — and does that beat move the skirt?

The character is the one the public guide
[rig-c/getting-started](https://github.com/rig-c/getting-started) builds in *Try it first*.
"Right" and "left" are the character's: the right leg is on the image's left.

## Verdict

**PARTIAL** — Through the producer, the stance can be done part of the way and no further. The
part that passed: one corrected keypoint (`l_ankle` moved onto the shin's art) makes
`rig-parts propose` draft `foot_r`/`foot_l` itself and the build stays green (check PASS), and
with that split the right ankle can slide **50 % of the way to parallel** (41.5 px, hip→ankle
15.96° → 12.08°) by two-bone IK with the skirt's first link keyed to follow the thigh, inside
every bar (hem gap 12 against 25, knee ghost 36 against 50, feet on the floor, no folds). The
part that failed: 75 % sits exactly on the hem bar (25) and 100 % tears the skirt (hem gaps 37
and 29, `bottomwear` folds); without the split every step fails from 25 % (the shin slides off
the painted shoe, ghost 290–462); and the feet never read as a true "11", because the right
shoe's toe-out is painted. The skirt does not answer the legs at all (0.00 px against the idle)
but the hip: on the cloth recipe a 6 px hip shift swings the left edge's tip link 6.68° and
moves the hem 5.62 px unkeyed (7.72 px in the passing beat, which also keys the first link), and

## Rejected alternatives

- **The unsplit rig (the guide's own build)** — fails from the first step: the painted shoe stays
  where it is and the shin slides off it (`shin_r|shoes` ghost 290 / 462 / 462 / 461) and the hem
  opens (gap 92 / 140 / 138 / 134). Splitting the shoes is required for any version of the beat.
- **IK without the skirt following (series a)** — the hem opens under the right thigh from 25 %
  (gap 86 / 136 / 141 / 134): the thigh turns about the hip, 180 px above the hem, and its art
  starts at the hem.
- **A rigid, straight leg (series r)** — keeps the knee clean (ghost 0) but lifts the ankle off
  the floor line, 5.3–17.2 px; with the skirt following (h) the hem holds (5 / 7 at 100 %) but
  the floor still fails.
- **Bending the knee outward** — the IK solution for the other bend starts with a jump at 0 %
  (`leg_r0` −7.70°, `leg_r1` +17.61° from rest), so it cannot start from the painted pose. Read
  from the solver's output (`o`, `q` series); these were never rendered.
- **Turning the shoe in the image plane (f, g)** — reads as a shoe tipped on its side, pushes the
  sole below the floor 13.0–24.3 px (f) and 18.4–39.3 px (g), opens `shin_r|foot_r` up to 28, and
  any foot turn beyond ±10° while a leg bone turns fails `FEET_STAY_LEVEL`. The toe-out is a yaw
  painted into the shoe.
- **`skirt_c0` following the thigh as well (k at ½, i at ¼)** — moves the opening to the left
  thigh: 72 and 33 at 75 % against 14 with `skirt_r0` alone.
- **A bigger hip shift (−12)** — doubles the skirt's response (13.28°, 11.26 px) and opens the
  left hem to 32.
- **The same beat on the plain split rig (no cloth recipe)** — builds green, but the hip move
  opens the left hem to 37. On the plain rig the passing version is the legs alone with
  `skirt_r0` following (j at 50 %), which leaves the skirt still.

## Limits

- One painting, one leg (the right) moving, one direction (toward the left leg's angle). A
  vertical target, a foot lift and a hip y move were not measured.
- Hold poses were read at 4 fps and the beats at 4 or 24 fps; a peak between frames is not read.
- The 7.72 px hem response of the passing beat is not the hip's share alone: its hip-still twin
  (ufol) solves the IK without the hip shift, so its `leg_r0` — and the `skirt0_0` key that
  follows it — is 10.03° against 11.11°. The 5.62 px of dbeat (no skirt key) is the cleaner
  figure for a 6 px hip shift.
- "Reads knock-kneed" at 75 % and "reads as a tipped shoe" are judgements by eye on the
  withheld crops, not measurements.
- The ankle caps are mostly missing (593 and 620 px) and the 402 px hole at the left ankle is
  there before the split. They stay hidden only while the foot stays level and the shin draws
  over it; a foot turned against the shin shows them (`shin_r|foot_r` gap 28 at a 44.4° turn).
- The commands of section 4 are reconstructed (see there); the commands of sections 1–3 and the
  re-reads of section 5 are as run.
- What a true "11" needs and this trial does not supply: a second painted right shoe turned
  toe-in (an `assemble.alternates` set switched by an attachment key), thigh art under the hem
  to go past about 50–75 % of the way, and a lower hip or a painting with the legs closer for
  both legs straight and parallel.
